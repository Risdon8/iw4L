# Skate 3 in MW2 :)

Skate around MW2 maps with Skate 3's physics, tricks and grinds.

## What you'll need

- The **Xbox 360 version of Skate 3** (the extracted `default.xex` with its `data` folder).
- **MW2** (only tested with the Steam version).
- A controller to skate.

## How to play

1. Grab the zip from [Releases](../../releases/latest) and extract it.
2. Double-click `iw4l.exe`, then point it at your MW2 folder and your Skate 3 `default.xex`.
3. Press **J** to skateboard.

More details in [docs/SKATE.md](docs/SKATE.md).
